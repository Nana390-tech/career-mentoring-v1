How this works: two sessions

This is career mentoring. It helps you plan your career and build a strong CV, even if you are not applying for a job yet. You meet your mentor twice, for 15 minutes each time. Session 1 gets you started. Session 2 is the follow-up, where you get feedback and your final copy.

Session 115-minute meeting with your mentor
  1. Talk with your mentor about the job you want.
  2. Your mentor shows you how to use this app.
  3. Start Steps 1–2 together, and try your 30-second introduction.
  4. Tap 💾 Save my work and book your Session 2 time.
Before Session 2 (at home): load your file, finish Steps 1–7, record your introduction and download your best take.
Session 215-minute follow-up with your mentor
  1. Bring your laptop or phone, your saved file, and your CV.
  2. Your mentor looks at your work and listens to your introduction.
  3. Write your feedback in Step 8 and your action plan in Step 9.
  4. Your mentor signs your work in Step 10.
  5. Download your final copy as a PDF for your portfolio.

Step 3 · Prepare at home

My professional summary

Formula: Who I am + What I bring + What I want.

Preview

Model: "Business student at HCT with a strong interest in human resources. Organised, a fast learner, and confident with people. Eager to apply my communication skills in an HR internship."
Don't copy the model. Use your own words and your own field.
Step 4 · Prepare at home

Three things I'm proud of

From college, work, volunteering, sport, clubs or a family business. Add a number if you can.

Step 5 · Prepare at home

CV self-check

Open your CV. Tick each item that is already correct. Your mentor will check it with you.

Free tools: ATS-friendly templates · ATS compatibility checker
Step 6 · Prepare at home

Bullet builder

Formula: Action Verb + Task + Result. Never start with "Responsible for…".

Before: Responsible for helping customers in my father's shop.
After: Served 40+ customers daily in a family retail business, improving repeat visits through friendly service.
Step 7 · Prepare at home

My 30-second introduction

Your mentor will say: "Imagine I'm a manager. Introduce yourself in 30 seconds."

My script

Model (B1): "Good morning, my name is Mariam. I'm a second-year Business student at HCT. I'm organised and I enjoy working with people. Last semester, I helped organise a college event for over 100 students. I'm looking for an internship in HR where I can develop my communication skills."

Time yourself

Aim for 25–45 seconds. Look up from the screen. Pause after your name and after your example.
