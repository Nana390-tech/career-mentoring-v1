Step 2 of 7At home

My career goal

What to do: Think about a job you would like in the future. You don't need to apply now. You will talk about it with your mentor.
Step 3 of 7At home

My professional summary

What to do: Write the 2–3 lines that go at the top of your CV. Use the formula Who I am + What I bring + What I want.

My summary

Model: "Business student at HCT with a strong interest in human resources. Organised, a fast learner, and confident with people. Eager to apply my communication skills in an HR internship."
Step 4 of 7At home

My experience and achievements

What to do: Write 2–3 things you have done: college, work, volunteering, sport, clubs or a family business. Then turn one into a CV bullet. When you finish, tap 💾 Save my work.

Turn it into a CV bullet

Formula: Action Verb + Task + Result. Never start with "Responsible for…".

Before: Responsible for helping customers in my father's shop.
After: Served 40+ customers daily in a family shop, improving repeat visits through friendly service.

🏠 Finished Steps 1–4?

Print or download Part 1: My Preparation and bring it to your mentor meeting. It has space for your mentor's notes. Then save your work.

Step 5 of 7With mentor

CV check and feedback

What to do: Look at your CV with your mentor. Tick each item that is already correct.

My mentor's feedback

Your mentor tells you; you write it in your own words.

Step 6 of 7With mentor

My action plan

What to do: Agree on up to 3 actions with your mentor. Then you both confirm.

Confirm the session

Step 7 of 7With mentor

My full report

What to do: Check your record, download the PDF, then upload it to the form. This is proof of your mentoring session.

Upload your record

  1. Tap ⬇ Download full report (PDF). Keep a copy for yourself.
  2. Open the upload form your mentor gives you.
  3. Fill in your details, upload the PDF and tap Submit.
  4. Show your mentor the "response recorded" screen.
